Warning signs this checker looks for

Urgency and fear

Scammers often demand immediate action or threaten account suspension to prevent careful checking.

Requests for secrets

Legitimate services should not ask you to reply with passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes.

Unexpected money requests

Gift cards, cryptocurrency, unusual transfer methods and surprise fees deserve independent verification.

Suspicious links

Displayed text can hide a different destination. Open the official service yourself instead of trusting the message link.

What to do after a warning

  1. Do not reply, click links or download attachments.
  2. Contact the organization through its official app or website.
  3. If you shared a password, change it from the official site and enable two-step verification.
  4. Report the message using your email provider’s phishing option.

Automated checks can miss sophisticated scams. A low score is not a guarantee of safety.
