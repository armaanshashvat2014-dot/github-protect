Ransomware

Ransomware is malware that blocks access to files or systems and demands payment. Prevention and reliable backups are especially important.

How risk is reduced

  • Keep the operating system, browser and applications updated.
  • Download software only from verified official sources.
  • Do not open unexpected attachments or installers.
  • Keep important files backed up separately and test recovery.
  • Use a standard account for everyday work when possible.

If something suspicious happens

Stop interacting with the file or message. Do not enter payment details. Disconnect affected devices from shared networks and ask a trusted adult, school IT administrator or qualified technician for help.
