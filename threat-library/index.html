Threat Encyclopedia

Clear explanations of common digital threats, how to recognize them and what safer behaviour looks like.

🎣 Phishing

Fake messages and websites designed to steal information or pressure you into unsafe actions.

👁 Spyware

Software that secretly collects activity or information without meaningful permission.

🔒 Ransomware

Malware that blocks access to data and demands payment.

⚠️ Fake updates

Imitation update alerts that try to deliver unwanted or dangerous software.

🧭 Browser hijacking

Unwanted changes to search, homepage, tabs or redirects.

Frequently asked questions

Can a browser tool replace antivirus software?

No. Browser tools can explain warning signs and inspect selected information, but full device protection requires trusted operating-system security software.

Does one warning sign prove something is malware?

No. Warning signs indicate that extra verification is needed. Decisions should consider the publisher, source, permissions, signatures and independent reputation.

What is the safest place to download software?

Use the developer’s verified official website or the device’s official application store, then confirm the publisher before installing.
