Phishing

Phishing uses fake messages or websites to impersonate a trusted organization and pressure people into revealing information or taking unsafe actions.

Common warning signs

  • The sender address does not match the claimed organization.
  • The message creates extreme urgency or threatens account closure.
  • A link uses a misspelling, unfamiliar domain or misleading subdomain.
  • It asks for passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes or unusual payments.
  • The greeting is generic even though the organization should know your name.

How to protect yourself

  1. Open the official app or type the known website address yourself.
  2. Check the complete domain before entering information.
  3. Use unique passwords and enable two-step verification.
  4. Report suspicious messages through the real service.
